#01 1st Arrondissement • 15 Place Vendôme

Ritz Paris

Imperial French Neoclassicism on Place Vendôme

Founded by César Ritz in 1898, the Ritz Paris stands as the definitive global benchmark of grand French art de vivre[cite: 30]. Beyond the bronze octagonal monuments of Place Vendôme[cite: 30], the property reveals meticulously preserved Belle Époque salons[cite: 30], wood-paneled alcoves where Proust composed masterpieces, and a subterranean Roman bath pool lined in hypnotic azure mosaics[cite: 30].

Bespoke Thermal Sanctuary

Chanel Spa Paris featuring private cabines and the subterranean azure Roman pool[cite: 30].

Nocturnal Libations

Bar Hemingway for iconic cocktails; Salon Proust for afternoon tea service[cite: 30].

#02 8th Arrondissement • Triangle d'Or

Four Seasons Hotel George V, Paris

Art Deco Grandeur in the Golden Triangle

An eight-story Art Deco monument reigning over Avenue George V since 1928[cite: 30, 31]. Revered for artistic director Jeff Leatham's monumental floral sculptures[cite: 31], the palace houses a sheltered marble courtyard and penthouses with private wrought-iron balconies framing unobstructed sunset vistas of the Eiffel Tower[cite: 31].

Haute Gastronomy (5 Stars)

5 Michelin stars under one roof: Le Cinq (3★), Le George (1★), and L'Orangerie (1★)[cite: 31].

Palace Hydrotherapy

Subterranean 17-meter mosaic lap pool, vitality pools, and bespoke anti-aging suites[cite: 31].

#03 16th Arrondissement • Avenue d'Iéna

Shangri-La Paris

Imperial Bonaparte Palace Above the Seine

The former residential palace of Prince Roland Bonaparte, blending regal French heritage with the gracious nuances of Asian hospitality[cite: 31]. Crowning the crest of Chaillot Hill, its premier suites deliver Paris's most intimate, cinematic terrace panoramas of the illuminated Eiffel Tower and winding Seine[cite: 31].

Chi The Spa

Daylight-flooded 15-meter subterranean swimming pool and organic garden terrace[cite: 31].

Distinguished Dining

Shang Palace (Michelin-starred Cantonese fine dining) and La Bauhinia under a glass dome[cite: 31].

#04 8th Arrondissement • Avenue Montaigne

Hôtel Plaza Athénée

Haute Couture Palace of the Dorchester Collection

Instantly distinguished by its 1,900 red geraniums and ruby canopies on Avenue Montaigne[cite: 31], Plaza Athénée is inextricably bound to French haute couture[cite: 31]. Its interior transitions gracefully between classic Louis XVI and refined Parisian Art Deco[cite: 31], accompanied by an ivy-clad courtyard that becomes an enchanting winter skating rink and garden salon[cite: 31].

Dior Spa Plaza Athénée

Haute couture wellness rituals, private sauna suites, and customized light therapies[cite: 31].

Courtyard & Salons

La Cour Jardin dining[cite: 31]; nocturnal designer cocktails at the electric Le Bar Plaza Athénée[cite: 31].

#05 8th Arrondissement • 30 Avenue George V

Bvlgari Hotel Paris

Italian High Jewellery Elegance on Avenue George V

Designed by Antonio Citterio Patricia Viel (ACPV)[cite: 32], Bvlgari Hotel Paris balances Italian marble splendor with Parisian understatement[cite: 32]. Clad in pale Vicenza stone with bronze louvers[cite: 32], the boutique sanctuary houses a private rooftop penthouse overlooking the monuments of Paris and a 25-meter golden mosaic pool reminiscent of ancient Roman therms[cite: 32].
